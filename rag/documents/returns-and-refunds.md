# Returns, Refunds, and Refund Timing

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes return eligibility, refund calculation, refund status,
and expected processing times for purchases made directly from Northstar
Supply. It applies to physical goods shipped to customers in the United States.
Marketplace purchases, subscriptions, gift cards, and orders placed through a
retail partner are outside this policy and must be routed to the original
seller or the specialized support queue.

This document explains policy. It does not establish whether a particular
refund was issued. For a customer's actual order or refund status, consult the
order and refund records. Never infer a completed refund from eligibility alone.

## 2. Key terms

- **Delivery date:** The carrier-confirmed date the package was delivered. If
  tracking has no delivery scan, use the date the customer received the item
  only after support verifies it.
- **Return window:** The number of calendar days after delivery in which a
  return request may be opened.
- **Refund initiated:** The refund was accepted for processing. This does not
  mean the bank or card issuer has posted the funds.
- **Refund completed:** The merchant's payment processor confirmed submission
  of the refund. Posting time after that point depends on the payment provider.
- **Original payment method:** The card, wallet, or other payment instrument
  used for the purchase. A refund cannot ordinarily be redirected to a new
  card on request.

## 3. Standard return eligibility

### 3.1 Window

Most unused or lightly handled items may be returned within 30 calendar days
of delivery. The request must be opened by 11:59 p.m. in the customer's local
time zone on day 30. The day after delivery is day 1.

### 3.2 Condition

An eligible item must include its standard components and must not show damage
caused by misuse, unauthorized repair, or avoidable exposure to liquid or heat.
Original packaging is helpful but is not required unless it is itself part of
the product (for example, a collectible presentation case).

### 3.3 Proof of purchase

Support must locate the order in the merchant's records. A receipt screenshot
alone is insufficient to authorize a refund if the order cannot be matched to
an account or transaction. Do not ask the customer to post full card numbers,
passwords, or authentication codes in chat.

## 4. Non-returnable and restricted items

The following are not eligible for a standard change-of-mind return:

1. Digital downloads after the download link has been used.
2. Gift cards, stored-value balances, and promotional credits.
3. Items marked **Final Sale** on the order confirmation.
4. Personal-care items whose factory seal has been opened, for hygiene reasons.
5. Custom-made products after production has started.

These restrictions do not remove any remedy that may be required for an item
that arrived damaged, defective, or materially different from its description.
Route those claims to the issue-resolution process in section 8 rather than
rejecting them solely because the item is on this list.

## 5. Refund amount and destination

For an approved return, the refund normally includes the item price and the
tax charged on that item. Standard outbound shipping is not refunded for a
change-of-mind return. If the merchant sent the wrong item or the item arrived
damaged, support may authorize reimbursement of reasonable return shipping
and the original shipping charge.

Bundle discounts are recalculated using the prices of the items the customer
keeps. A partial bundle return may therefore produce a lower refund than the
standalone list price of the returned item. Do not calculate a bundle refund
from list prices; use the order's recorded line-item allocation or ask the
refunds team to review it.

Refunds are sent to the original payment method. If that account is closed,
the customer should contact the card issuer or payment provider; support must
not promise that the merchant can select a different destination.

## 6. Processing states and timing

### 6.1 Merchant processing

After the returned item is received and inspected, allow up to 5 business days
for the merchant to approve and submit an eligible refund. Some low-value
items may be refunded without a physical return; only the order record or an
explicit support authorization can confirm that exception.

### 6.2 Payment-provider posting

Once the merchant's system records the refund as completed, banks and payment
providers commonly take 5–10 business days to display the credit. This is an
estimate, not a guarantee. The merchant cannot accelerate the issuer's
posting process.

### 6.3 How to answer status questions

Use the refund record for the actual state and timestamp. Distinguish clearly
between “requested,” “processing,” and “completed.” If the available tools do
not expose a refund record or its timestamp, say that the current information
does not show it and escalate or look it up. Do not claim that no refund exists
because an order-status response omits refund details. Do not state a specific
completion date based only on the estimates in this policy.

## 7. Late or missing refunds

If the refund is marked completed but the credit is not visible after 10
business days, ask the customer to check the statement for the original payment
method and contact the issuer. If the issuer cannot locate it, support should
open a payment trace with the refund ID and completion timestamp. Never request
the customer's full card number for this trace.

If the merchant record says pending, failed, or contains no refund entry,
route the case to the refunds team. Do not tell the customer to wait through
the bank-posting estimate until merchant submission is confirmed.

## 8. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

Ask for the order identifier and a concise description of the issue. Photos may
be requested through the approved secure upload flow; they are not required in
ordinary chat. Support may offer replacement, return, or refund after checking
inventory and the relevant order. A replacement and a full refund must not be
issued for the same unit unless a supervisor explicitly approves it.

## 9. Exceptions and escalation

Escalate for human review when the request is outside the window but involves
a verified delivery failure, a safety issue, a repeated defect, suspected
payment fraud, a disputed bundle calculation, or a mismatch between the order
and refund records. Escalation is a review, not a promise of approval.

## 10. Examples

- **Within window, unopened keyboard:** Eligible for a standard return if the
  purchase is found and no Final Sale restriction applies.
- **Refund marked completed yesterday:** Tell the customer the merchant
  submitted it yesterday; explain that issuer posting can take an estimated
  5–10 business days. Do not say the funds have already appeared.
- **Order endpoint has no refund timestamp:** Check the refund record or
  escalate. The missing field is not evidence that the refund did not happen.
- **Opened sealed personal-care product, reported defective:** Route as a
  defect claim; do not apply the ordinary change-of-mind restriction as the
  final decision.

## 11. Policy precedence

Applicable law and a documented supervisor decision take precedence over this
sample policy. A specific written promotion may override the standard return
window only for the items and dates named in that promotion. Do not invent an
exception when none is recorded.
